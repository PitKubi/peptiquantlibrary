About the PeptiQuant Library

The PeptiQuant Library lists the {{ total }} synthetic proteotypic peptides synthesized and quality-controlled by MRM Proteomics and the Borchers laboratory: stable-isotope-labeled standards (SIS) and their unlabeled (NAT) counterparts for targeted quantitative proteomics (MRM / PRM). Each peptide carries its synthesis QC record: mass confirmation by TTOF and purity assessment by CZE and amino acid analysis.

Search the catalog for peptides of your proteins of interest and request a quote directly from the results. Peptides not in the catalog can be synthesized on demand.

The catalog is curated and kept current by the MRM Proteomics team.
