About the PeptiQuant Library

The PeptiQuant Library lists {{ total }} synthetic proteotypic peptides covering {{ proteins }} proteins, synthesized and quality-controlled by MRM Proteomics and the Borchers laboratory: stable-isotope-labeled standards (SIS) and their unlabeled counterparts (NAT) for targeted quantitative proteomics (MRM / PRM).

Search by gene, protein or sequence, or paste a whole target list, then add peptides to your list and request a quote. Peptides not marked available can be synthesized on demand.

The catalog is maintained by the MRM Proteomics team.
